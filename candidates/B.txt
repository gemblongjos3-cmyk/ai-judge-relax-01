The river is low this evening, and it carries one leaf the long way around the bend. It does not hurry. It turns in the slow water, catches a little light, and goes on turning. On the bank, the grass has gone soft with dew. A heron stands in the shallows with one leg folded, doing nothing at all, and doing it beautifully. The sky above the far trees is the colour of a bruise healing — blue going quiet into grey. There is no sound except the water finding its way around the stones, the way it always has. It will still be finding its way tomorrow.
